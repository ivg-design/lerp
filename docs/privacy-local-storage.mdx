# Privacy (localStorage)

LERP is designed to run without accounts or server-side learner profiles. Course progress stays in your browser.

## What LERP Stores

LERP stores learning progress in your browser `localStorage`, such as:

- completed lessons
- quiz and exercise progress
- user interface preferences

This data stays on your device.

## Aggregate Website Measurement

Forge separately counts selected website actions in aggregate, such as starting the course or opening an external resource. These counts do not move your course progress out of `localStorage`. See the [Forge measurement disclosure](https://forge.mograph.life/measurement/) for what is counted.

## What LERP Does Not Store

LERP does not store:

- account identities
- emails or personal profiles
- payment information
- server-side activity logs tied to a user

## Data Export and Reset

If available in your current build, use the in-app export/reset actions to back up or clear local progress data.
You can also clear site storage through browser settings.
